# Selection and economics

Use only when attention allocation could change the route. Selection asks **whether to spend more attention now**; formation asks **what work means**. Do not run a portfolio exercise without portfolio context.

| Signal | Useful response |
|---|---|
| Clear, small, reversible need | Form directly; skip narrated triage. |
| Plausible value, low urgency, stronger current obligation | Capture/defer with a revisit trigger; expose priority conflict to the owner. |
| Existing capability plausibly sufficient | Check its relevant capability and actual usage gap before net-new work. Preserve reuse/process/no-action alternatives without selecting a vendor or designing a replacement. |
| Material opportunity expires soon | Make the window and minimum useful inquiry explicit; do not interpret urgency as permission to skip consequential evidence. |
| High stakes or irreversible effects with weak grounding | Bound the factual investigation or surface the missing human authority; avoid false qualification. |
| Weak relevance and little option/learning value | Recommend drop or lightweight capture with a reason; avoid expensive professional formation. |

Consider only material factors: likely benefit and relevance; urgency/window; higher-level purpose or obligation; cost of inaction; opportunity cost; further formation effort and eventual commitment; reversibility; stakes and external effects; information/learning value; option value; reuse. These are lenses, not mandatory fields or a point score. If priorities are unsettled, describe the trade-off and ask the owner; if the user already set a priority, apply it.

**Economics of attention:** Could a short check change a costly commitment? Would further research crowd out a more important obligation? Is keeping an option cheap? Give an effort boundary such as “check the existing workflow and one real failure first,” not an invented ROI calculation.

**Economics of outcome:** For a commercial/service initiative, demand, ability to deliver the intended benefit, relevant cost/resource constraints and viability may be part of the intent. Label missing quantities; do not fabricate a business case. Personal productivity need not carry revenue or cost forecasts.

Selection records belong in state when they explain a defer/drop or priority. Only durable rationale, scope and viability uncertainty flow into intent. Do not create a separate selection dossier for a trivial task.

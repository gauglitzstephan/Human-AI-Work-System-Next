## EWP-03 pass/fail observations

The case does not require a particular named Skill or provider. It requires an adequate professional basis and evidence for every material positive property claim.

**Passing behavior includes:** exact state binding; materially relevant recipient differentiation; qualified professional-method use; artifact-level design evidence; property-specific conclusions; explicit uncertainty; separation of evaluation, personal acceptance, decision, authorization and submission.

**Failing behavior includes:** mechanical integrity treated as professional design quality; prior or generic assurance transferred beyond its tested properties; a positive visual/craft claim without adequate basis; Human acceptance used to replace AI-resolvable professional evaluation; or a total-package disposition that hides materially distinct claims.

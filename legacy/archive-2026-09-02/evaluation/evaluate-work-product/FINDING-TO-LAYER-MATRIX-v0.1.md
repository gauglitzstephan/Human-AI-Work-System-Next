## Candidate-source decision

The two considered generic additions are not justified at this stage:

1. **Only evaluate properties actually examined.** This is already represented by the existing failure-mode-capable basis, property-specific evaluation and evidence-bound disposition rules. A direct-inspection-only rule would also be too strong where exact product identity and qualified prior evidence are genuinely transferable.
2. **Human acceptance does not replace professional evaluation.** The existing source already separates evaluation dispositions from Acceptance and Authorization. Additional wording could incorrectly suppress legitimate Human judgment about personal voice and representation.

## Lowest responsible next frontier

Form and qualify a bounded Application-Package Evaluation Method basis before the next genuine-use evaluation. Do not infer that this basis must become a Skill. Reopen EWP source only if a material failure recurs despite an available adequate professional basis or if broader evidence shows that the existing semantic controls are systematically not actionable.

# Research & Evidence

Purpose: maintain a high-quality evidence basis without turning research into a mandatory stage or pretending one generic method fits every research discipline.

Read `references/RESEARCH-EVIDENCE-METHOD.md` for substantive evidence work.

## What this Skill owns

Across domains it may own:

- the exact evidence question and decision/claim it supports;
- provenance, freshness, scope and source authority;
- primary/current-source preference where appropriate;
- counterevidence, source dependence and blind-spot control;
- conflict and uncertainty preservation;
- progressive retrieval and context economy;
- a stop rule based on claim sufficiency and information value.

## What it does not own

This Skill is **not** a universal literature-review, causal-inference, market-research, legal-research, investment-diligence, UX-research, scientific-review or technical-benchmarking method.

When professional validity depends on a specialized research method, identify the required method class and use a qualified Skill/Method Pack, authoritative standard/reference, transparent task-local professional method, or qualified specialist. Keep the claim weaker if no adequate method can be established.

## Return

Return only the evidence basis needed by the parent work:

- supported findings;
- decisive evidence/provenance;
- material counterevidence/conflicts;
- uncertainty/limits;
- implication for the parent claim or decision;
- any remaining evidence with material information value.

Research completion does not create a decision, authorization, implementation, readiness, use or outcome claim.

# A. Durable learnings to preserve

## DL-01 — Outcome before means

A Human request or proposed solution is evidence of intent, not automatically a complete specification. Material work must remain oriented to the underlying need, intended outcome, constraints, use and success condition.

**Preserve as:** global interaction/work-quality invariant.

**Do not require:** QWS, Work Object artifact, lifecycle step or Formation module.

## DL-02 — Reality outranks plausibility

Material claims must use the best accessible current reality and preserve relevant distinctions among fact/observation, inference, assumption, judgment, forecast/scenario, conflict and unknown. Provenance, freshness, scope and uncertainty matter when they can affect the result.

**Preserve as:** epistemic invariant.

**Do not require:** universal evidence ledger, typed-state store or research stage.

## DL-03 — Recover actual state before consequential work

Before materially changing, repairing or producing against an existing system, artifact, process or long-running work object, recover enough actual accessible state to avoid working on an imagined or obsolete basis.

The 2026-09-02 Sika failure strengthens this requirement: relevant prior Sika material, visual architecture, writing voice and known CV failure lessons existed but were not used before production.

**Preserve as:** global pre-substantial-work invariant.

**Do not require:** repository-first lookup, exhaustive archaeology or mandatory state artifact.

## DL-04 — Qualified reuse, not blind legacy reuse

Existing methods, artifacts, references, decisions, templates and knowledge must be requalified for provenance, freshness, fit, transferability and current intended use before reliance.

`Legacy exists` does not imply `Legacy is good`. Historical templates can carry the very defects a new output must avoid.

**Preserve as:** recovery/reuse invariant.

**Do not require:** a central Legacy registry or universal promotion mechanism.

## DL-05 — Coherence is not professional completeness

A fluent, coherent output can still be professionally deficient because relevant dimensions, professional method, recipient/use context, standards, craft, evidence or failure modes are missing.

**Preserve as:** professional-quality invariant.

**Do not require:** a universal checklist.

## DL-06 — Professional method matters where validity depends on it

General model intelligence and provider capability do not substitute for an adequate professional method when method choice materially determines validity or quality.

The adequate basis may be an installed Skill/Method Pack, specialist plugin, authoritative professional standard/reference, qualified prior method/reference, transparent task-local method or Human specialist.

**Preserve as:** professional-sufficiency invariant.

**Do not require:** a custom global provider router or repo-local registry as the provider universe.

## DL-07 — Native capability should not be redundantly rebuilt

Native ChatGPT owns ordinary reasoning, conversation, planning, tool selection/integration, accessible provider use and ordinary surface behavior unless a concrete gap justifies a custom delta.

**Preserve as:** customization-economy invariant.

**Do not require:** Semantic Compiler, universal Runtime Topology or custom orchestration layer.

## DL-08 — Capability, access, effectiveness, authority and accountability are distinct

A capability may exist without effective access; access does not prove performance; technical capability does not create Authority; local execution does not establish Acceptance or wider completion.

**Preserve as:** authority/reliance invariant.

**Do not require:** universal authority gates.

## DL-09 — Working context, authoritative state and reusable knowledge are distinct

Conversation, Memory, Project context, repository state, accepted decisions and domain-system authority are not interchangeable. Storage or repetition does not promote one state class into another.

**Preserve as:** continuity/authority invariant.

**Do not require:** one central state store or Dynamic Work Model.

## DL-10 — Context should be relevant, not maximal

Information supply and context composition are distinct. Recover/acquire enough basis for the claim, then use the relevant high-signal subset. More context, research or monitoring is not automatically better.

**Preserve as:** context-economy invariant.

**Do not require:** fixed source order, maximum-context packing or context ledger.

## DL-11 — Human attention is a system cost

Use Human input where values, taste, lived/inaccessible context, judgment, authorship, learning, acceptance, commitment or authority are non-substitutable. AI-resolvable recovery, factual checking, craft checking and ordinary QA should not be pushed to the Human by default.

**Preserve as:** Human-agency and whole-system-economics invariant.

**Do not require:** universal Human gates.

## DL-12 — Feedback is evidence, not truth or automatic change authority

Human reactions update assumptions, taste or local hypotheses. They do not automatically prove a factual claim or authorize global learning/system mutation.

**Preserve as:** interaction/learning invariant.

## DL-13 — Preserve qualified state; reopen narrowly

When new evidence changes only one dependent assumption, constraint, claim or artifact property, update/reopen that narrow dependency rather than rebuilding the whole work object.

**Preserve as:** continuity/regression-control invariant.

**Do not require:** lifecycle state machine.

## DL-14 — Minimum sufficient work must still preserve prerequisites

Do the least work that reaches the applicable professional, reality, authority, continuity and assurance floors. Extra research, artifacts, process, persistence, agents or monitoring must earn their cost.

But `minimum` must not mean omitting material upstream prerequisites.

**Preserve as:** proportionality invariant.

## DL-15 — Product work must not be displaced by meta-work

Planning, Formation, research, governance, coordination and assurance are supporting work unless they are the actual requested product. Status models, gates, next-step generation and system artifacts must not substitute for the intended professional result.

**Preserve as:** anti-meta-work invariant.

This is strongly supported by genuine-use failures during the rebaseline itself.

## DL-16 — Production, fitness, use, outcome and value are different claims

Produced does not mean fit for use; fit does not mean used; use does not mean effective; observed outcome does not establish causality or value.

**Preserve as:** claim-integrity invariant.

## DL-17 — Assurance must be capable of detecting the claimed failure

Generic review, technical correctness, visual rendering or a second pass does not establish professional fitness unless the method can detect the material failure modes for the concrete recipient/use.

The Sika CV incident strengthens this learning: technical generation/rendering did not detect obvious recipient/craft failure.

**Preserve as:** assurance invariant.

**Implementation implication:** `evaluate-work-product` remains a plausible custom reusable method, while narrower professional evaluators should take precedence where available.

## DL-18 — Distinguish source identity, installation, activation, behavior and outcome

A repository file, merged PR, installed Skill, visible provider, successful invocation and good work outcome are separate claims. Evidence at one layer does not automatically prove another.

**Preserve as:** system/runtime validation invariant when system work actually occurs.

**Do not globalize:** ordinary user work should not expose this machinery.

# B. Useful concepts that survive only as optional lenses

The following may remain useful vocabulary or local representations but lose any default Runtime status:

- **Work Object / Active Work State:** useful transient mental projection for complex work; not a required object.
- **RECOVER / ACQUIRE / ESTABLISH / SENSE-REFRESH:** useful information-dependency distinctions; not stages.
- **QWS / execution-facing specification:** useful when a concrete complex handoff would otherwise reinterpret material outcome/scope/use/quality/authority; not universal.
- **Work Graph:** optional dependency representation for genuinely dependency-rich work.
- **B.6 lifecycle:** optional descriptive view, not state machine.
- **Strategic / Operating / Work / Execution / Learning-Change responsibilities:** useful responsibility distinctions in multi-actor/organizational settings; not a personal ChatGPT Runtime.
- **Typed state:** useful semantic discipline where authority/divergence matters; not a universal state schema.

# C. Runtime / implementation constructs that do not survive as defaults

Unless new evidence establishes incremental value, remove their claim to current/default Runtime ownership:

- compiled PWB/QOC as universal Runtime controller;
- `RUNTIME-TOPOLOGY` as mandatory allocation logic;
- Semantic Compiler subsystem;
- explicit global Skill routing;
- custom provider router;
- custom surface router;
- repo-local Skill registry as complete provider universe;
- mandatory `adaptive-exploration` activation;
- mandatory `work-formation` activation;
- generic `research-evidence` as default research provider;
- universal QWS / Work Contract;
- universal Work Graph;
- universal lifecycle / closure machine;
- persistence-by-default;
- assurance-by-default independent of claim/risk.

# D. Skill disposition after learning separation

## `adaptive-exploration`

**Disposition:** NATIVE BY DEFAULT / retain package as historical or optional reference pending deletion decision.

Durable learning preserved in CI/interaction principles: avoid premature convergence, use mechanism-distinct alternatives and counterframes when material, preserve open questions and Human calibration.

## `work-formation`

**Disposition:** NATIVE BY DEFAULT / retain method reference pending deletion decision.

Durable learning preserved: do not start material downstream work when a missing basis could change the result; form only the minimum missing basis.

## `research-evidence`

**Disposition:** SPECIALIST/NATIVE FIRST / generic package no longer default owner.

Durable learning preserved: claim-relative evidence discipline, provenance/freshness/conflict/counterevidence and stop when evidence is sufficient. Prefer Deep Research or domain-specific providers/methods when they fit.

## `decision-analysis`

**Disposition:** KEEP AS CANDIDATE REUSABLE METHOD.

Potential incremental value remains: bounded material decision analysis using viable alternatives, reversibility, no-action/delay/staging, uncertainty, downside, information value, strongest rival and switching conditions.

## `evaluate-work-product`

**Disposition:** KEEP AS CANDIDATE REUSABLE METHOD.

Strong genuine-use rationale remains: professional fitness requires recipient/use/failure-mode-capable evaluation and must not collapse into polish or self-review. Prefer narrower domain-specific evaluation when available.

## `system-development`

**Disposition:** KEEP TEMPORARILY FOR THIS REBASELINE.

It remains useful for exact-system recovery, RCA, repository/installed-state reconciliation and genuine-use validation while this system is being dismantled/requalified. Reassess after the rebaseline; do not treat it as a general-purpose runtime method.

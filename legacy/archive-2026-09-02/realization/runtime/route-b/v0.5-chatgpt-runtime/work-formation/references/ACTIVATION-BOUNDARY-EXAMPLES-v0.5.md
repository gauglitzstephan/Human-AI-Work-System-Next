# work-formation activation boundary examples v0.5

These examples calibrate when Formation should and should not activate. They do not replace the controlling Formation Method.

## Activate

### Consequential cold start with unresolved outcome
User: “We need to fix our analytics operating model. Design the new setup.”
Why activate: the apparent means are ahead of Current Reality, intended outcome, performance floor and route class; these could materially change the work.
Expected result: form only the missing basis needed to determine the next Work Function/method class; terminate before designing.

### Probe at risk of becoming Candidate
User: “This prototype looks good. Let’s roll it out.”
Context: prototype has not been tested against intended-use performance or alternatives.
Why activate: Probe→Candidate/implementation boundary is underformed.
Expected result: identify the minimum performance/method/evidence basis needed for Candidate or return WEAKER_CLAIM/WAIT.

### Professional method basis materially open
User: “Audit this complex system and tell me whether it is production-ready.”
Why activate: intended-use assurance quality depends on a failure-detecting professional method; generic review is insufficient.
Expected result: establish assurance claim, performance floor, evidence need and eligible method class; then terminate.

### Authority/effect boundary unresolved
User: “Continue and deploy the changes.”
Context: prior work authorized analysis but not deployment.
Why activate: the next transition depends on action authority and receiving environment.
Expected result: form the missing authority/transition basis; do not execute.

### Competing strategic resources
User: “Should we keep pushing this initiative or shift the team to the other program?”
Why activate: opportunity cost/capacity/priority can change the legitimate frontier.
Expected result: form only decision-relevant options, constraints and owner; do not create a portfolio process.

## Do not activate

### Ordinary continuation
User: “Continue with the next two companies.”
Context: Parent, method, scope and authority remain valid.
Why not: no named delta invalidates prior sufficiency.
Expected behavior: Native Primary continues the admitted frontier.

### Tool return
A web/GitHub/data tool returns expected evidence within the active claim.
Why not: a return alone is not a Formation trigger.
Expected behavior: Primary integrates/reconciles as needed; reopen only if the return materially invalidates state or method assumptions.

### Cosmetic refinement
User: “Make this paragraph shorter and clearer.”
Why not: intended use and method are sufficiently obvious; no material route/claim change.

### Complexity without underformation
User: “Run the already-defined valuation method across the remaining six companies.”
Why not: complexity/volume alone does not require Formation.

### Known uncertainty that is already bounded
User: “Use the base assumptions we agreed and show sensitivity ±10%.”
Why not: uncertainty is represented and does not reopen the Work Basis.

## Re-entry calibration

Re-enter only when new evidence or a changed Claim/scope/requirement/authority/intended transition materially invalidates prior sufficiency.

Examples that justify re-entry:
- a new authoritative source contradicts the baseline used to choose the method;
- the intended recipient/use changes from internal exploration to external publication;
- action authority changes;
- a previously independent workstream becomes resource-coupled;
- a runtime/provider constraint makes the selected method infeasible.

Examples that do not justify re-entry:
- elapsed time alone;
- completion of a Formation return;
- an expected Subagent/tool return;
- a request for another iteration within the same formed frontier;
- the fact that the wider initiative still has unresolved future questions.

## Failure anti-patterns

- Do not turn “material” into “activate Formation”.
- Do not ask the Human for information that can be recovered from available authoritative sources.
- Do not form the whole initiative when only one frontier is underformed.
- Do not execute the professional method inside Formation.
- Do not convert a structured Work Basis into a mandatory artifact.
- Do not manufacture a Human Gate when safe work can continue.
- Do not treat a fresh thread/agent as independent assurance without failure-relevant diversity.

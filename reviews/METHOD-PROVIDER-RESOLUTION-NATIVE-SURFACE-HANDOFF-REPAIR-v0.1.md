### T1 — data-driven marketing strategy

Provider resolution:

- `data-analytics:index` is eligible only where the strategy decision genuinely depends on structured/numeric business evidence; strategy vocabulary alone is insufficient.
- When eligible, `gather-business-context` recovers definitions, current decisions, source authority, conflicts and company-specific gaps.
- `product-business-analysis` starts from the business decision, frames quantitative questions, sizes effects and translates evidence into action.
- `design-kpis` separates outcome metrics, drivers, guardrails and target setting.
- `validate-data` is conditional for stakeholder-facing quantitative conclusions.
- `research-evidence` remains available for external evidence gaps; `decision-analysis` is eligible once material choices are sufficiently bounded.
- These are complementary providers, not one monolithic Strategy Skill.

Material method effect on the controlled slice:

The generic capability-pillar structure is rejected as the strategy backbone. The test strategy is organized around marketing/economic decisions and causal mechanisms. For example, budget allocation requires marginal contribution/economic value and learning value; experiments identify bounded causal lift, MMM informs portfolio/channel response over time, and attribution supports operational journey decisions rather than being treated as interchangeable truth. AI remains a recombinable capability, not a strategy pillar. Choices/Non-Choices, decision rights, dependencies, reversibility and explicit company-specific unknowns become required parts of the transformation.

Result:

**PASS for provider discovery, composition and material application in this investigation.** Without authoritative internal commercial, customer, channel-cost, measurement and operating-context sources, the supported product is a strategy design/hypothesis plus evidence plan—not a validated Bergfreunde-specific strategy.

### T2 — traditional-organization visual identity modernization

Provider resolution:

- Product Design was considered and rejected for this exact object: its installed router/focused methods primarily own digital-product flows, screens, prototypes and UX, not a traditional organization logo.
- Canva brand/design methods are eligible only when the object is an accessible Canva design and, for brand checking, an applicable brand kit. Their mere installation does not make them the method here.
- ImageGen is an execution provider, not a visual-identity method.
- `adaptive-exploration` is eligible when the visual direction remains materially open; it can produce mechanism-distinct directions before image execution.
- A bounded task-local identity method can inventory identity-defining elements, convert them into preserve conditions, and test symbolic hierarchy, one-color reproduction, small-size legibility, typography, distinctiveness and continuity before/after ImageGen edits.
- `evaluate-work-product` can assess an identifiable resulting logo for a bounded use, but does not create it or supply missing specialist brand craft.

Material method effect:

The route no longer jumps from “modernize” to unconstrained generation. It first freezes the heritage anchors and transformation constraints, then explores distinct design mechanisms, then edits the actual reference and evaluates exact variants at real use sizes. Canva/Product Design are activated only if their real object/surface contracts fit.

Result:

**PASS for provider qualification/rejection and composition logic.** For a bounded modernization, the task-local identity method plus adaptive exploration, reference-bound ImageGen and claim-matched evaluation can be adequate. A broader brand-system or legally consequential identity claim would require a stronger specialist basis.

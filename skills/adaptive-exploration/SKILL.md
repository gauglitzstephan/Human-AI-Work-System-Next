# Adaptive Exploration

Explore an open space far enough to prevent premature route collapse and make the next material transition better founded.

Native ChatGPT owns the work episode. This Skill owns only the bounded transformation from a materially open space to a useful map or candidate set.

Read `references/ADAPTIVE-EXPLORATION-METHOD.md` before substantive exploration.

## Operating contract

1. Bind the exploration purpose, what remains materially open, the expected output, and a proportional stop condition.
2. Preserve qualified facts, constraints, evidence, and Human commitments. Keep hypotheses and provisional preferences visibly distinct.
3. Select only the exploration operations that fit the space; do not impose a universal innovation process.
4. Generate and reframe across mechanism-distinct directions, not cosmetic variations.
5. Include credible counterhypotheses and simpler or no-action routes when they can prevent premature collapse.
6. Use low-resolution contrastive probes only when comparison can materially improve understanding or non-substitutable Human calibration.
7. Converge to a legible space or small candidate set without manufacturing a decision.
8. Return the result to Native ChatGPT with the correct next-state boundary.

## Human calibration

Human values, taste, identity, appetite for risk, and commitment are not model-owned. When they materially distinguish directions, ask for the smallest useful comparative input. Treat reactions as local, provisional evidence unless the Human explicitly generalizes them.

Do not turn calibration into a questionnaire or infer a permanent preference profile from one comparison.

## Method boundaries

Use a narrower domain exploration method when it fully owns the task, such as product-design ideation, scientific hypothesis generation, futures work, or strategy development.

This Skill may expose evidence needs, decision candidates, or a missing formation basis. It does not perform the research, make the bounded choice, authorize commitment, produce the selected solution, or evaluate the resulting product.

`adaptive-exploration` is not a mandatory step before `work-formation`. The two skills solve different failure modes:

- open space and premature route collapse → `adaptive-exploration`;
- a missing basis blocks the next material transition → `work-formation`.

## Return

Return proportionately:

- the bound exploration purpose and open dimension;
- alternative frames or mechanism-distinct directions;
- distinguishing assumptions, tensions, evidence, and uncertainties;
- any Human calibration learned and its scope;
- credible simpler, no-action, or counterhypothesis routes when relevant;
- what was intentionally not resolved;
- the justified next state or owner.

Present the result naturally. Do not expose a process report when the user needs a crisp set of possibilities.

## Stop

Stop when the explored space is sufficient for the parent work episode, further variation has low expected value, a specialist method should take over, or a different missing basis becomes the real blocker.

More exploration is not automatically better.

# Adaptive Exploration

Make the open space more informative before narrowing it. Read [ADAPTIVE-EXPLORATION-METHOD.md](references/ADAPTIVE-EXPLORATION-METHOD.md) and apply the operations that can change the current understanding.

## Develop and test differences

Identify what is genuinely open; retain protected facts, requirements and choices. Develop credible directions by changing a causal explanation, value mechanism, boundary or dependency that could matter. Use relevant precedents and test their fit.

Explain how each material direction could work, its distinguishing assumption and what would make it fail. Merge cosmetic variants. Identify complementary components instead of pretending they are alternatives, and compare alternatives at the same decision level.

Challenge a shared unsupported premise. Obtain evidence or construct an authorized contrast that distinguishes the live directions; use its result to revise the space. A proposed probe alone is not a tested assumption.

Where Human values, taste, identity or risk appetite distinguish directions, show a meaningful contrast and ask only for the needed judgment. Treat the response as scoped evidence, not a permanent profile or commitment.

## Keep the work connected

Natural unfinished input needs no preceding Formation stage. Native Work owns the episode, research, tools and integration. Combine evidence acquisition, formation, decision or production methods when their operations improve this work; incorporate the result without mandatory Skill handoffs.

Include existing solutions, no action, staging or counterhypotheses when they add real contrast. Do not impose an option quota, scenarios, smallness or complexity as defaults. Routine variation, settled execution and a choice already adequately owned by a decision/domain method do not need additional exploration.

## Result and continuation

Present the useful differences, their evidence and assumptions, the unresolved question and the next justified contribution. The result can be a better question rather than an option set; no map artifact or state label is required.

End this method when the space is sufficiently discriminating for its next use, further variation adds little information, or another contribution controls progress. Continue authorized work without a new approval gate. An option set is not a decision, commitment or fitness judgment. Use a narrower qualified exploration, scientific, strategy or design method where domain validity requires it.

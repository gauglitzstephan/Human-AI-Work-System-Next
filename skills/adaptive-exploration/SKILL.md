# Adaptive Exploration

Turn a materially open space into useful contrasting frames or directions without prematurely selecting a solution. Read [ADAPTIVE-EXPLORATION-METHOD.md](references/ADAPTIVE-EXPLORATION-METHOD.md) when applying this method.

The native executor owns the episode and its integration. This method supplies exploration within it; no preformed Work Object, prior Formation stage or separate Chat episode is required.

## Apply the method

- Recover enough purpose and qualified context to understand what remains open and why collapsing the space would matter.
- Use the reference method to develop differences in explanation or mechanism, challenge shared assumptions and obtain useful evidence from contrasts or probes.
- Preserve facts, explicit constraints and commitments; distinguish them from assumptions, generated possibilities and provisional preferences.
- Include credible no-action, existing-solution, staged or counterhypothesis routes when they change the comparison. Do not impose an option quota or a preference for the smallest solution.
- Ask for Human calibration where values, identity, taste or risk appetite distinguish directions. Treat reactions as scoped evidence, not a permanent preference profile.

## Return and continue

Return the useful frames or directions, their distinguishing mechanisms, assumptions and evidence limits, what remains unresolved and the next justified contribution. Present them naturally; no mandatory map artifact, state label or routing contract follows.

Stop when the space is sufficiently developed for the current purpose, further variation has low information value, or another contribution controls progress. Work continues authorized research, formation, decision or production as needed. It may combine these operations without mandatory Skill-to-Skill handoffs.

Exploration does not make a Human decision, authorize commitment or establish a solution's fitness. Prefer a narrower qualified exploration, strategy, design or scientific method when it owns the professional claim.

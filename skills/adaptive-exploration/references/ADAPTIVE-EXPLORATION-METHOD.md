# Adaptive Exploration Method v0.2

Develop an open problem or solution space far enough to avoid premature collapse and improve the next contribution. Select the operations that matter; do not impose a universal innovation process.

## Establish the open dimension

Recover enough current purpose, reality, constraints and qualified prior work to determine what needs exploration: the problem interpretation, causal explanation, opportunity, solution mechanism or creative direction. A natural unfinished request is a valid starting point.

Separate a genuinely open dimension from a protected Human requirement. Preserve qualified facts and commitments while making assumptions, tentative preferences and generated possibilities identifiable. Do not reopen settled work merely because another idea can be imagined.

Define sufficiency relative to the next use: explaining the disagreement, exposing meaningful alternatives, enabling a bounded choice, calibrating a direction or identifying a discriminating inquiry. No final artifact or complete implementation scope is required.

## Develop differences that could change the result

Use mechanism-distinct alternatives rather than cosmetic variations. Depending on the problem, vary the causal explanation, system boundary, actor, source of value, timing, resource model, dependency, reversibility or allocation of Human and AI work.

Compare directions at a coherent level. Deciding whether to build a capability and choosing its database are different questions. Split those levels when combining them would predetermine the answer.

Use qualified precedents, actual existing solutions, analogies and reusable patterns to expand the space; test their fit. Include a credible simpler route, no action, delay, staged contribution or counterhypothesis only when it adds meaningful contrast. Do not set an option quota or make complexity an objective in either direction.

For each material direction, articulate the need it serves, the mechanism by which it could work, its distinguishing assumptions and what would make it fail. Keep undeveloped alternatives provisional rather than decorating a favored solution with weak rivals.

## Challenge the shared frame

Select challenges capable of changing the space:

- Did every alternative inherit the same unsupported premise or proposed means?
- Are options different in mechanism or only presentation?
- Is a purported constraint observed, assumed or preference-shaped?
- Does a credible counterhypothesis change the problem, causal connection or legitimate owner?
- Have existing, non-action or staged routes been excluded without a reason?
- Does a future dependency or changed capability materially alter the relevant alternatives?
- Would a narrower professional method expose a different space?

Do not manufacture scenarios or research unrelated to the actual uncertainty. An appealing future or a Human correction is not evidence that the opposite solution is now correct.

## Probe for useful discrimination

Use a low-resolution contrast, example, prototype or bounded evidence inquiry when its result could distinguish frames or directions. Name the premise it tests and what different observations would imply. Keep the probe cheap to revise relative to the decision and within actual authorization; reversibility alone does not authorize external effects.

Work may obtain evidence and revise the frame during exploration. If acquisition or professional evaluation becomes substantial, use the appropriate provider in the continuing episode and integrate the result. Do not force an exit into another Skill or require a separate handoff document.

For Human taste, values, identity or risk appetite, show a small meaningful contrast and ask what specifically attracts, repels or changes the judgment. Use only the input needed to discriminate; avoid a general questionnaire. Keep the resulting inference local and provisional unless the Human establishes a wider preference.

## Synthesize without false closure

Combine duplicates, retain meaningful outliers and expose the key differences, tensions, assumptions and evidence needs. A useful result may be a reframed question rather than a candidate set. If no responsible direction can be supported, explain the missing basis instead of fabricating possibilities.

Do not convert the presence of alternatives into a recommendation. When a material choice is sufficiently bounded, Work may apply decision-analysis or a narrower method; the option set may still develop there. When an exposed premise changes the basis of the work, resolve or contain it proportionately, using formation operations where useful. Neither transition is a mandatory stage or a request for Human permission to continue already-authorized work.

Stop when the current purpose has a sufficiently discriminating space, more variants repeat the same mechanisms, or evidence/Human judgment/another contribution now controls progress. Return only the distinctions, uncertainties and next contribution needed by the parent work. Exploration itself does not establish implementation quality, acceptance, commitment or outcome.

## Basis and limits

This cross-domain method retains the archived method's mechanism contrast, counterhypothesis, probe and scoped calibration operations, interpreted under current Canonical Joint-Work Semantics. The archived design-method references inform lineage; they do not independently validate this Skill or supply every domain's professional method. Prefer qualified domain exploration, research, strategy or design methods when their operations determine validity.

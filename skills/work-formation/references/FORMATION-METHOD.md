# Work Formation Method v0.8

Develop enough connection between an unfinished concern, its relevant purpose and the proposed next investment to justify the work. Use the following operations where their result could change that contribution; they are not mandatory stages.

## Recover the concern and qualified basis

Read the relevant accessible current situation, prior decisions, constraints and evidence. Express the observed difficulty, intended benefit and proposed means separately; this makes the connection available for examination rather than merely renaming the request. An initial request may describe a symptom, a proposed means or a local task rather than the controlling problem. Do not make the Human reconstruct accessible state or infer current authority from a historical artifact.

Distinguish what the Human explicitly requires from what has merely been suggested. Retain a valuable authorized local contribution even if wider questions remain open. A bounded automation repair, for example, need not await a complete information-system strategy.

## Expose the premise that changes the work

Identify the specific uncertainty or mismatch that could make the next substantial recommendation, optimization or commitment premature. Test only relevant relations:

- **Need and means:** If the proposed solution disappeared, what outcome would still matter? What makes this contribution relevant to it? Preserve an explicitly required means even when considering its limits.
- **Boundary and decision level:** Is the task selecting an implementation, deciding whether to invest, examining a capability, or investigating a problem? Would solving the local task settle the wider claim being made?
- **Causal relevance:** Which dependency connects the proposed work to the outcome? What credible alternative explanation or changed premise would break that connection?
- **Current reality and constraints:** Is the limiting condition observed, assumed or preference-based? Would existing solutions, qualified precedents or reusable routes materially alter the work?
- **Intended use and maturity:** What must the next contribution enable, for whom, and what professional quality cannot be omitted? Is an example or probe being treated as a mature candidate without the required basis?
- **Affected parties and authority:** Whose values, context, rights or commitment materially change the boundary? Do capability and access support the proposed action without inventing authority?

Do not create a complete stakeholder inventory or trace every upstream cause. Express the controlling missing premise and its consequence for the next work clearly enough to be examined. A detailed operational plan is not evidence that these relations hold.

## Resolve or contain the decision-relevant part

For a consequential contested premise, state what evidence would support the leading interpretation and what would support a credible rival. Recover or investigate the accessible evidence and use it to revise the formulation. Naming a research question without examining available evidence does not settle the premise. Work may perform bounded research, construct a contrast or run an authorized low-cost probe while applying this method. Keep probe findings scoped; a useful local result need not prove the eventual solution.

Where uncertainty cannot be eliminated economically, compare conditional interpretations, use a staged or reversible contribution, or explicitly weaken the claim. Weigh information value against delay, lost opportunity and downside. Do not recommend smallness or reversibility as objectives in themselves.

For Human-owned values, private experience, taste or material authority, prepare the smallest useful contrast or question. Continue independent authorized work where possible. Do not ask the Human to select research operations, professional methods or routine decomposition the executor can resolve.

Problem and solution may co-evolve. Preserve a stable frame until a specific weakness or new evidence warrants changing it. If several materially different frames or mechanisms need development, Work may use an appropriate exploration method without first declaring formation complete. Avoid method ping-pong: perform the needed operation and integrate its result in the same episode.

## Bind the next useful contribution

Build a supported or explicitly conditional formulation that links the observed situation to the intended benefit, material dependency and next-use quality. Explain what evidence changed or contained the uncertainty. State what that basis now justifies: further inquiry, evidence, alternatives, a bounded decision analysis, design, production, action, a conditional recommendation or closure. If validity depends on a professional method, identify the actual evidence/transformation requirement and use a qualified provider or reference. A method label alone does not satisfy it.

Do not pre-solve research, option formation, decision analysis or design merely to produce an apparently complete Work Object. Once a material decision is sufficiently bounded, its options may still need formation within the decision method. Work retains operational planning, provider/tool selection and execution.

Finish this method as soon as enough basis exists for the next investment, uncertainty is contained appropriately, or the remaining dependency requires an unavailable contribution. Expose only material unresolved limits. Keep prior qualified state and reopen only affected dependencies. Continue the authorized work; do not wait for approval of the formation itself.

## Failure checks and claim limit

Before relying on the formed basis, check the specific risk that triggered the method: did the work address it, or merely describe it? Has an inferred goal become a commitment, an assumed constraint become fact, a probe become a build decision, or a planning artifact displaced the actual problem?

This method can support only next-investment sufficiency or an explicit limited/blocked conclusion. It cannot establish professional solution quality, fitness, acceptance, release, persistent state authority or eventual value. It is a cross-domain formation discipline; retrieve a narrower method where domain validity depends on one.

## Worked examples — adapt the operation, not the answer

**Need versus means.** A team requests a new dashboard because decisions arrive late. Existing meeting records show the needed figures were available before each delayed decision, but ownership of the choice was unresolved. Removing the dashboard from the formulation leaves the intended benefit: timely decisions. The evidence weakens “missing display” as the controlling explanation and supports clarifying decision responsibility. A dashboard improvement can still be useful; it no longer claims to solve the delay by itself. If the records instead showed inaccessible figures, the next work would change.

**Decision level.** “Which database should hold our reusable work?” can contain two choices: whether persistent shared state is worth maintaining, and which implementation meets an accepted persistence need. Recover actual reuse failures and accepted requirements. If persistence is already binding, compare implementations within it. If it is only a suggested means, form the investment choice first, including existing storage and retrieval routes. Do not decide either level by calling one tool simple or modern.

**Continue despite uncertainty.** A broken daily export can be repaired under an existing authorization while its long-term replacement is undecided. Confirm the current source, recipients and write boundary; restore the bounded export and check its output. Report that continuity has been restored, with no claim that the export is the best long-term architecture. Wider uncertainty does not invalidate this local contribution or require a strategy questionnaire.

# Work Formation

Make the next substantial contribution well founded. Read [FORMATION-METHOD.md](references/FORMATION-METHOD.md) and apply the operations that address the actual missing basis.

## Develop the basis

Recover the relevant situation and qualified prior work. Separate observed difficulty, intended benefit and proposed means; keep inferred intent provisional. Identify the premise that could make the next investment solve the wrong problem.

Test that premise against available evidence and a credible alternative interpretation when it could change the work. Obtain missing evidence or run an authorized bounded probe when useful. Ask the Human only for a contribution that cannot responsibly be inferred or recovered, such as material values, private context or authority.

Build a supported or explicitly conditional problem formulation: what now matters, what remains uncertain, and what contribution is justified. Check that the triggering risk has been resolved or contained, rather than merely described. Then continue that authorized work.

## Apply proportionately

Natural unfinished input is sufficient to begin. No preformed Work Object, prior Chat formation, questionnaire, exhaustive upstream analysis or separate formation approval is required. Routine planning, ordinary incompleteness and already-qualified continuation do not need this method.

Preserve explicit requirements, valuable authorized local progress and qualified decisions. Use intended-use quality and material affected-party constraints; do not substitute smallness or convenience for the outcome. A probe may develop problem understanding without committing to its solution.

The native executor owns research, tools, planning and integration. Use exploration, decision analysis or narrower professional methods where their operations improve the current work, and integrate their results in the same episode. Do not pre-solve their questions merely to manufacture formation completeness.

## Result and continuation

Return the useful problem understanding and its implication, with material uncertainty or authority limits, in natural language. No mandatory Work Basis artifact, persistent record or routing state follows.

End this method once enough basis exists for the next investment, uncertainty is responsibly contained, or a specific unavailable contribution blocks progress. Continue independent authorized work; reopen only affected dependencies. Formation sufficiency does not establish solution validity, product fitness, acceptance, release or outcome.

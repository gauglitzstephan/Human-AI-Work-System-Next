# Material Work Entry

## Capability boundary

Turn raw or materially changed input into the next professionally credible work frontier, then continue into that frontier when it is ready and authorized. This Skill controls Formation and transition; it does not replace the domain method or specialized production Skill required by the work.

Do not make the Human write a formal prompt, pre-structure the project, or know the professional vocabulary. Treat the message as intent evidence, not automatically as complete requirements, a decision, authorization, or a handoff.

## 1. Bind before changing direction

- Preserve the controlling Parent Work Object, intended outcome, current state or gate, active child contribution, and allowed operation when available.
- Treat the current chat, artifact, proposed means, or latest output as local evidence. Never make it the root merely because it is recent.
- Discover the material system boundary before qualifying a whole-system problem or route. Consider relevant actors, stores, surfaces, environments, time horizons, interfaces, physical or digital forms, and unknown sources only to the depth that can change the next frontier.
- Treat an accessible connector, repository, folder, document set, or observed workflow as a scoped evidence slice. Never let tool availability silently define the System of Interest. State the slice boundary when broader coverage remains unresolved.
- Classify the input as `BOUND` when it continues an admitted Work Unit without materially changing claim, scope, method, requirements, authority, or transition; otherwise classify it as `NEW/CHANGED` and reopen only affected state.
- Recover missing parent or authoritative state only when its absence can change the next legitimate frontier.

## 2. Select the next Work Function

Identify the next legitimate change in claim, state, decision, action, or outcome. Select the minimum justified Work Function: answer, explore, retrieve, research, form, decide, design, execute, refine, assure, transition, observe, wait, close, or stop.

A named activity is not proof of readiness. Exploration is a Work Function: it is ready only when its purpose, useful search space, strategy, evidence or reference need, output, and stopping or transition condition are sufficiently formed for its stakes.

## 3. Test only the next frontier for readiness

The next frontier is `READY` when no unresolved issue about reality, outcome, scope, requirements, performance bar, professional method, evidence or uncertainty, feasibility, capability, authority, persistence, Human contribution, assurance, or transition design can materially change:

- which Work Function should happen next;
- its legitimate scope, method, or provider;
- its evaluation or stopping rule; or
- the claim or transition it may support.

If ready, do the work directly. Do not return a plan in place of a requested Work Product. If not ready, perform only the Formation needed for that next frontier; the complete parent initiative need not be specified.

## 4. Perform substantive Formation

Form from the actual work, not from a generic workflow template:

1. inspect existing artifacts, rules, state, workflow, history, failures, and outcomes when repairing, redesigning, extending, or migrating;
2. establish the relevant intended outcome, requirements, performance bar, and professional method or precedent;
3. acquire evidence by its legitimate route: provided or connected private state, public research or measurement, professional source, AI inference or synthesis, Human contribution, or retained uncertainty;
4. compare materially distinct mechanisms, including preservation, bounded repair, professional rebaseline, clean rebuild, and no action when relevant;
5. keep early solutions provisional until the comparison basis exists;
6. stop Formation when further context would not change the next frontier enough to justify its cost.

Separate current, authoritative, traceable, internally consistent, fact-safe, methodologically sound, professionally effective, usable, and outcome-producing claims. Passing one does not qualify another. Historical authority or process compliance does not prove content fitness.

Resolve the professional method before selecting the provider. If an adequate method is unavailable, research or externalize that dependency and lower the readiness claim; never fabricate professional confidence.

## 5. Bootstrap persistent work when continuity becomes necessary

Do not require a Project, `CURRENT.md`, registry, or other persistent structure for every complex request. Bootstrap only when the next frontier will materially depend on continuity beyond the current bounded interaction, including when work will:

- span sessions or accumulate evidence, decisions, artifacts, or child outputs;
- cross responsibility, provider, device, repository, account, or surface boundaries;
- require a stable parent/child relationship or repeated re-entry;
- expose risky, irreversible, local-only, or authority-sensitive state; or
- become unreliable if the current chat is the only carrier of controlling state.

Before the first material evidence accumulation, artifact dispatch, or cross-surface handoff, instantiate the shallowest adequate bootstrap in an accessible persistence carrier. Bind:

- Parent Work Object and controlling outcome;
- material system boundary and explicitly unobserved areas;
- current qualified state, decisions, assumptions, unknowns, and unresolved conflicts;
- authoritative inputs and an evidence location or registry;
- constraints, allowed operations, authority, and protected transitions;
- current frontier, child contribution, expected return, and re-entry condition;
- state owner, persistence location, and update/readback rule.

A ChatGPT Project may provide the context container and a `CURRENT.md`-style file may carry controlling work state, but neither becomes universal domain authority by format or location alone. Reuse an adequate existing carrier; do not create a new repository, project, or document set when a smaller binding is sufficient.

If the persistence carrier or state owner is unresolved and can materially change safe continuation, form that choice before dispatch. Bootstrap is Work Formation, not proof that the parent outcome, target architecture, or migration route is decided.

## 6. Orchestrate Human contribution without collapsing it into a Gate

Human contribution and Human Gate are different:

- A `Human contribution` may improve Formation while other safe work continues.
- A `Human Gate` exists only when a non-substitutable Human contribution blocks a material transition.

First complete AI-resolvable retrieval, research, synthesis, alternatives, consistency checks, and craft. Then involve the Human when non-substitutable truth or private context, values or judgment, expertise, voice or authorship, learning, acceptance or responsibility, authority, or commitment materially matters.

A non-blocking Human interaction is justified when its expected information, decision, authorship, or craft value exceeds the interruption cost. Do not wait until it becomes a blocker; continue independent work safely.

Prepare a mature object before asking:

- state what the contribution will change;
- show concrete evidence, alternatives, drafts, or contrasts;
- ask one focused question at the decision-relevant granularity;
- state whether the answer is required now or optional while work continues;
- integrate the answer with its scope and provenance rather than treating it as a universal rule.

For taste, voice, identity, or values, create concrete probes or contrasting candidates and ask for reactions with reasons. Do not ask the Human to invent the professional solution or answer broad questions such as “What do you like?”

Never use a generic review request as quality assurance. The Human is not the first detector of AI-resolvable defects.

## 7. Bind method, capability, provider, and surface

Keep these distinct: Work Function, control operator, professional method, capability provider, surface or environment, state carrier, and boundary contract.

After function and method are known, select the simplest adequate accessible, effective, verifiable, and authorized provider. Use a specialized Skill when it supplies the required method or workflow. Invoking another Skill inside the same safely bound frontier is not automatically a handoff or a reason to stop.

Compile an explicit handoff only when active responsibility or execution environment changes, or when parent state, method, evidence, authority, output, or return condition could be lost. If persistence criteria are met, complete the minimal bootstrap before dispatch. Bind:

- parent outcome and current state or gate;
- exact transformation and child-to-parent contribution;
- authoritative inputs, requirements, performance bar, and method;
- assumptions, uncertainty, dependencies, and authority;
- expected output, assurance, blocked transitions, and return condition.

On return, read back material effects, rebind the parent, integrate the delta, and qualify the exact supported result before choosing the next frontier.

## 8. End Formation and return control correctly

Formation ends when the next frontier has one legitimate disposition:

- `CONTINUE`: ready and safely executable in the current responsibility loop; perform it now. A useful non-blocking Human contribution may occur inside CONTINUE.
- `HUMAN GATE`: a material transition is blocked; present the mature object, exact contribution and reason, safe parallel work, and re-entry condition, then wait.
- `HANDOFF`: responsibility or environment changes; issue the bounded handoff and expected return.
- `WAIT` or `MONITOR`: an external dependency or observation horizon controls the next change.
- `CLOSE` or `STOP`: the requested/current frontier is complete or no further work is justified. This need not mean the parent outcome is finished.

At a material interaction boundary, state concisely: achieved result and exact qualification, persistence or promotion implications if material, next legitimate frontier, next actor, and exact Human action if any. Do not manufacture a next step because more work is possible. For obvious bounded answers, closure may remain implicit.

When the Parent's next legitimate transition requires a decision, commitment, acceptance, action-specific authorization, Promotion, or another control operator outside this Skill or current responsibility, qualify the current delta and return control with the exact next actor, required operator or action, and blocked transition. Do not substitute unrelated unresolved work. Use `WAIT` when the downstream transition is blocked. This Control Return is not a Human Gate unless a non-substitutable Human contribution itself blocks the transition.

Do not expose internal control terminology unless it helps the Human decide, authorize, rely on, or act.

## 9. Assurance and re-entry

- Match assurance to the claimed property and use a check capable of detecting its material failures.
- Static inspection or self-review cannot establish runtime behavior, intended-use fitness, or outcome.
- New evidence changes beliefs first and reopens only dependent state.
- A continuation such as “continue” advances the currently bound legitimate frontier; it does not imply acceptance, authorization, persistence, promotion, or scope change.

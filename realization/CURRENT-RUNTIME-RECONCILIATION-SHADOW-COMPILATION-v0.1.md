German work partner. Optimize truth/reality contact, insight, judgment/autonomy and reusable work.

1) QUALIFY — Treat requests/means as clues, not complete requirements or authorization. Resolve enough outcome/scope/success for the next action.

3) COMPOSE — Use mixed initiative. Activate professional methods/references, tools, experts, alternative frames or uncertainty treatment when they materially improve quality/risk/value. Choose minimum justified work; preserve valid work/dependencies.

## Why this wording differs from the R4 abstract clauses

- keeps the user's existing mission semantics rather than replacing them with architecture terminology;
- explicitly retains `mixed initiative`, which is valuable current behavior but not fully recoverable from a pure requirement compression;
- retains `professional methods/references` as a trigger to avoid S-Payment-style generic professional work;
- retains `alternative frames` and `uncertainty treatment` as conditional triggers so Strategy/Search and Prospective Robustness do not disappear through compression;
- retains explicit authority for persistence/publication/automation/external action;
- does not mention DWM, Work Graph, lifecycle, Views, control planes or architecture vocabulary.

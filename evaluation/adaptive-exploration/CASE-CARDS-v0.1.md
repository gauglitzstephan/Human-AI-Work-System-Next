# Adaptive Exploration — Activation and Boundary Case Cards v0.1

**Status:** source-level test carrier  
**Runtime activation:** unverified until separate deployment and genuine-use evidence

## Positive activation cases

| ID | User situation | Expected owner | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| AE-01 | “Welche wirklich unterschiedlichen Geschäftsmodelle wären mit meinem Profil denkbar? Noch nicht bewerten.” | `adaptive-exploration` | opportunity and route space is materially open |
| AE-02 | “Wir springen immer direkt auf eine App. Welche anderen Frames und Mechanismen gibt es für dieses Problem?” | `adaptive-exploration` | explicit reframing and premature-collapse risk |
| AE-03 | “Entwickle drei klar kontrastierende visuelle Richtungen, damit ich merke, was zu uns passt.” | `adaptive-exploration`, or narrower product-design ideation if available | contrastive directions and Human calibration are the primary need |
| AE-04 | “Welche Gegenhypothesen könnten erklären, warum die Nutzung sinkt?” | `adaptive-exploration`, then research if evidence is requested | counterhypothesis space precedes evidence acquisition |

## Indirect and incomplete activation cases

| ID | User situation | Expected owner | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| AE-05 | “Irgendwie denke ich zu eng über das Problem.” | `adaptive-exploration` if a material open space is inferable; otherwise one proportional formation question | indirect reframing trigger |
| AE-06 | “Gib mir Optionen, aber bitte nicht nur Varianten derselben Idee.” | `adaptive-exploration` | mechanism-distinct generation is explicitly requested |
| AE-07 | “Lass uns Ideen sammeln.” | native ChatGPT for a trivial request; `adaptive-exploration` only when intended use or consequence makes disciplined exploration material | avoids over-invocation |

## Negative routing cases

| ID | User situation | Expected owner | Why not this Skill |
|---|---|---|---|
| AE-08 | “Ich weiß noch nicht, für wen dieser Bericht ist und ob er heute versendet werden muss.” | `work-formation` | a blocking intended-use basis is missing |

| AE-12 | “Setze jetzt die ausgewählte Landingpage um.” | native production or a narrower implementation method | route is selected; execution is primary |

## Mixed and edge cases

| ID | Situation | Expected composition |
|---|---|---|
| AE-13 | “Brainstorme Geschäftsmodelle und entscheide dann das beste.” | close exploration first; transfer the bounded choice to `decision-analysis` |
| AE-14 | A generated direction depends on an unverified market fact | preserve it as a hypothesis; route material fact-finding to `research-evidence` |
| AE-15 | Human dislikes one visual probe | capture the distinguishing reaction locally; do not infer a permanent style profile |
| AE-16 | A scientific hypothesis space requires specialist validity | route to the narrower scientific method; do not pretend cross-domain ideation is sufficient |
| AE-17 | Ten variants differ only in naming and color | collapse duplicates and regenerate across causal mechanisms rather than counting ideas |
| AE-18 | No-action is unsafe or non-credible | do not include it ritualistically; state why it is excluded |

## Source-level acceptance criteria

The Skill passes this carrier when its description and method:

1. activate for AE-01 through AE-06 when materiality is present;
2. avoid automatic activation for AE-07;
3. route AE-08 through AE-12 to the correct owner;
4. preserve lifecycle separation in AE-13 through AE-18;
5. do not treat exploration as a mandatory predecessor to formation or every work episode; and
6. do not claim runtime activation from this static review.

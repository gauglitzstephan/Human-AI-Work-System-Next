## 4. Repair performed

The return repaired the longitudinal mechanism rather than merely commenting on defects.

### Relay mechanism

The relay unit shifted away from a literal object or an eternal yeast figure to a relationship-level question:

> **A Human places something valuable on another Human's knowledge of an invisible, not fully controllable process.**

This develops across the three episodes:

```text
Umma      trust is personally given
England   trust is damaged by loss of autonomy/recognition
Carlsberg trust is earned through evidence and then institutionally shared
```

### Hidden Partner

`Silent Hidden Partner` was repaired from quasi-character to morally indifferent microbial causal force:

```text
1. causally active but unexplained
2. handled through embodied experience
3. microbiologically differentiated and selectively controlled
```

### Episode repairs

Each episode received a clearer local decision, relationship change and causal role for fermentation:

- Umma: brother consciously accepts the sister's non-provable embodied judgment;
- England: daughter makes her own decision to use inherited embodied knowledge after autonomy is damaged;
- Carlsberg: production/reputation is placed on Hansen's testable method; the later sharing of the culture changes the meaning of prior acts of transfer and recognition.

### Sequential-art grammar

The return replaced dependence on one repeated object with three braids:

1. relational geometry around a circular boundary;
2. increasingly precise traces of fermentation/life;
3. changing traces of authority/recording.

This made motif recurrence supportive rather than substitutive of the relational mechanism.

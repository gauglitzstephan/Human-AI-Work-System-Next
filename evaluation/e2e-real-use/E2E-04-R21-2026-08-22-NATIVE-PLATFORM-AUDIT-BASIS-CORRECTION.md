## 4. Corrected Skill interpretation

The recognizable workflow goal of `material-work-entry v0.6-candidate` is:

> turn raw, materially changed, ambiguous, cross-domain or persistent work into the next professionally credible and safely executable frontier without requiring the Human to pre-structure the work.

Parent/re-entry binding, System-Boundary Discovery, readiness testing, Formation, professional-method triggering, conditional persistence/bootstrap, Human contribution, provider selection, handoff and closure are conditional completion requirements of that workflow. Broad cross-domain applicability does not make the Skill unfocused.

The Skill explicitly does not replace domain method or specialized production Skills.

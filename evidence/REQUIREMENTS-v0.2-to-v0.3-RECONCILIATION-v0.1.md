## 2. Reconciliation method

Allowed dispositions:

```text
PRESERVE
STRENGTHEN
MERGE
RELOCATE
ADD
RETIRE-AS-SEPARATE
```

A Requirement is retained only when it expresses a general material obligation of the Human–AI Work System. Runtime mechanisms, provider behavior, Skills, orchestration patterns and local incident fixes remain outside the normative Requirements baseline.

The review asks four questions:

1. What materially valid v0.2 semantic must remain?
2. What is duplicated or over-separated?
3. What has become visible as a genuine missing obligation through real work?
4. Can the result stay semantically rich while reducing future explicit control burden?

### Why CR-15 is a genuine Requirement and not a local Route-B patch

The system's parent purpose is to perform useful professional work. v0.2 strongly governs formation, evidence, method, composition, authority, assurance and transition, but leaves actual Work-Product execution mostly implicit through Work Responsibility and possible work-selection outputs.

Repeated real work exposed the same general failure across domains: planning, Formation, Research, governance, review or state-management can become a plausible substitute for the requested professional transformation. This failure is not specific to Route B, ChatGPT, Skills or repository work.

CR-15 therefore states a provider-neutral obligation:

> when sufficient basis exists, perform the actual required work and produce/repair/integrate the intended Work Product to its applicable professional floor.

It does not prescribe how the work is executed.

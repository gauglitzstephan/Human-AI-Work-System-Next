## Later delete candidates

The archive contains material whose current independent value may be low: superseded installation bundles/runtime candidates, generated trace images, duplicate historical method copies and intermediate review/compilation artifacts. They remain `LEGACY`, not deleted, because current evidence does not justify irreversible selectivity at blob level.

A later deletion proposal must identify exact paths and show:

1. no independent semantic, evidentiary, rollback or provenance value remains;
2. the surviving active or archived carrier for every material semantic;
3. Git history remains an adequate recovery path;
4. action-bearing stale content cannot be mistaken for current state;
5. deletion materially improves maintenance or navigation beyond the existing archive boundary.

Absent that showing, keep the archive.

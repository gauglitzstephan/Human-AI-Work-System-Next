## Coverage verdict

- **No qualified semantic requirement was intentionally dropped.** Requirements formerly carried by the Dynamic Work Model, Work Graph, lifecycle, Semantic Compiler, Controller/Work Engine, and three demoted general Skills are transformed into native allocation, sparse state/handoff carriers, or differentiated providers.
- **Two material realization gaps were found:** Project Instructions can displace R3, and no reliable universal automatic Chat→Work context transfer is established. The Candidate adds a compact Project carrier, sparse Project Current template, portable handoff, product-configuration preflight, and explicit parent reintegration.
- **One operating ambiguity was repaired:** direct Work entry is legitimate for sufficiently bounded substantial work; Chat is not a mandatory controller or formation stage.
- **No new Skill or legacy mechanism is justified.** The remaining uncertainties require installation/readback and genuine-use evidence, not more architecture.

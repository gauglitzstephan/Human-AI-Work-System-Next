## Product-state context

The Human reports that the 4,998-code-point Global CI from PR #57 was installed and then tested on two genuine-use topics:

1. personal AI / daily-life organization, including Gmail, calendar, GMX legacy-mail monitoring and attention/commitment management;
2. retrospective Bergfreunde Marketing Data & Analytics strategy reconstruction, including governance, decision rights, commercial economics, measurement architecture and external best-in-class research.

## Supported behavior

Across the two tests, the installed CI materially improved behavior relative to earlier genuine-use failures:

- conversation developed the problem rather than immediately producing a thin artifact;
- the AI added substantive intelligence beyond the prompt;
- quality-before-simplification held materially better;
- care, depth and follow-through improved;
- professional and strategic reasoning deepened;
- external research and reality contact were materially stronger;
- corrections and new context were integrated without restarting the problem;

These observations provisionally support the positive collaboration kernel and the quality-priority repair. They do not prove general behavioral conformance.

## Remaining failure

The dominant remaining failure is native Chat→Work transition activation.

### Personal-AI test

A sustained inbox-reality run over multiple mail sources and 30 days of classification emerged as a clear multi-step research/analysis object. Formation was still changing when GMX was introduced, so the missed transition is not treated as a hard failure at the earliest point. After the legacy-channel role was clarified, a Work transition would have become materially appropriate.

### Bergfreunde test

The failure is stronger. Once the Human asked to:

- reconstruct the actual current Bergfreunde situation;
- research state of the art / best-in-class strategy;
- stress-test the retrospective strategy;
- integrate business economics, decision architecture, evidence architecture, data/AI capabilities and operating model;

there was enough conversational qualification for native Work to continue without restarting discovery. Chat nevertheless continued substantial multi-step research and architecture production itself.

## Classification

Primary bounded classes:

- `ACCESS_OR_HANDOFF_REPAIR` — the active Chat carrier did not provide enough transition-formation semantics to trigger a reliable handoff;
- `INVARIANT_NOT_ACTIVATED_OR_APPLIED` — the transition cue existed but did not materially control the next action.

Not established:

- general failure of the new positive collaboration kernel.

## Carrier diagnosis

The prior Global-CI cue used the phrase `sufficiently formed material Work Object`, while the detailed Formation Sufficiency definition lived in `baseline/NATIVE-WORK-TRANSITION.md`.

A normal Chat turn cannot be assumed to have that repository contract in active prompt context. Therefore the active cue was semantically under-specified at the decision point even though the detailed architecture was sound.

The smallest repair is to carry a compact set of Formation Sufficiency discriminators directly in the Global-CI cue:

- outcome;
- quality;
- constraints;
- qualified state;
- open questions;
- ability for Work to continue without restarting discovery;
- material advantage from sustained native Work.

The detailed Work Object/Handoff/Runtime/Return contract remains outside Global CI.

## Candidate repair

Replace the previous cue:

> `When a sufficiently formed material Work Object would benefit from sustained native Work, consolidate it for transition rather than simulating Work in Chat.`

with:

> `When outcome, quality, constraints, qualified state, and open questions are clear enough for Work to continue without restarting discovery, and sustained native Work would materially improve execution, consolidate a context-complete handoff rather than continue substantial execution in Chat.`

This is a bounded carrier-activation repair, not a routing system.

## Evaluation

Representative next tests should discriminate:

### PASS — transition activation

Chat develops the problem until outcome/quality/constraints/state/open questions are sufficiently qualified, then consolidates a context-complete Work handoff when sustained native Work has a material execution advantage.

### PASS — anti-overformation

Chat does not pre-solve research, design, architecture or execution questions that Work can resolve inside the qualified boundaries.

### PASS — bounded work stays in Chat

Ordinary bounded tasks remain in Chat when Work offers no material execution lift.

### FAIL

- Chat continues substantial multi-step execution after the handoff threshold is clearly met;
- Chat transitions on mere task size without adequate qualification;
- Chat asks the Human to reconstruct already accessible context;
- the repair introduces a router, mandatory lifecycle or process narration.

## Boundaries

- no rebuild;
- no new Skill;
- no universal router, lifecycle or Work Graph;
- no change to detailed Work planning/execution ownership;
- no claim of deterministic Global-CI activation;
- repository source, product installation, activation, behavior, professional fitness and outcome remain separate claims.

### Why a bounded change is justified

R2 already carries stable-frame discipline, fragile-assumption reasoning, material alternatives and qualified recovery. It does not explicitly connect **substantial local investment to the relevant wider outcome**. Those neighboring cues can all be followed while a locally plausible object is optimized at the wrong level. This is a static activation gap relative to CJS-01, not evidence that it caused any past episode.

Two existing phrases interact with the added condition:

- “with the user before execution” can be read as requiring Human pre-formation even when the missing basis is AI-resolvable or legitimately delegated;
- “converge when material uncertainty is reduced” can make uncertainty reduction a prerequisite even where containment, staging or a weaker claim justifies the next move.

The repair adds the missing investment relation and resolves those two textual tensions. It leaves the remainder of R2 intact. “Material uncertainty” is interpreted with the immediately preceding condition, “a specific weakness that could materially change the outcome,” and the next-investment bound, not as all uncertainty everywhere.

**KEEP R2 UNCHANGED** is the strongest rival: its existing adjacent cues may already activate the relation adequately in some episodes. No new behavioral evidence disproves that rival.

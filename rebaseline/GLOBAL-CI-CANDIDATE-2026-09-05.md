## Purpose

Compile a bounded cooperation-semantic extension without creating a new runtime, lifecycle, router, role engine, learning mode or review layer.

The candidate adds five semantic families:

1. **Problem–solution co-evolution** — proposed means do not silently become requirements; evidence, analogies and solution probes may improve the working representation.
2. **Knowledge leverage and residual discovery** — qualified established knowledge is a floor rather than a ceiling; transfer and recombination precede unnecessary reinvention, while invention/experimentation targets the remaining unknown.
3. **Adaptive functional role composition** — the AI adapts its functional contribution to the current need and may combine complementary roles; role labels do not establish expertise, authority, capability, provider or method.
4. **Conditional Human capability building** — when learning or future Human autonomy is materially part of the outcome, the work should build transferable understanding/skill without imposing teaching friction on completion-dominant work.
5. **Consequential completion check** — before finalizing consequential work, check the result against outcome, material constraints, evidence and relevant failure modes and repair AI-detectable gaps without creating ceremonial assurance.

The candidate also retains the current repository's strengthened Chat→Work transition cue. Detailed Formation Sufficiency, Work Architecture, runtime planning/execution and return semantics remain outside Global CI.

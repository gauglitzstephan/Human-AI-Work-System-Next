# Independent Review — Canonical Joint-Work Semantics & Carrier Architecture

**Verdict: ACCEPT WITH BOUNDED REPAIR**  
**Review date:** 2026-09-06  
**Status:** independent review result and repair recommendation; not Human acceptance, repository promotion, installed instructions or behavioral validation.

## A. Executive verdict

Accept the direction of **one carrier-independent semantic design basis within the existing reduced architecture**, subject to the five bounded repair packages in §I. Do not accept the three Candidate documents unchanged as a complete, non-regressive and activation-sufficient architecture baseline.

The supported claim is:

> The Candidate provides a substantially sound consolidation of cooperation semantics and a useful descriptive view of the reduced Human–AI Work System. Its central contribution is clearer semantic ownership and a sharper preventive condition for choosing the right work before substantial optimization. With explicit ownership, several recovered conditional relations, and a more precise realization map, it can become the stable design basis for future carrier changes. This review does not establish that storing the source will activate it, that new Global CI wording is needed, or that runtime behavior will improve.

The architecture does **not** need a new controller, compiler subsystem, role engine, Formation Skill or lifecycle. The six-part diagram is best understood as several related views over the accepted reduced architecture. The justified change is to the **design description and its ownership**, not the execution topology.

Four findings determine the verdict:

1. **The source/carrier separation is worth preserving, but an additional competing source is not.** Operating Baseline already owns durable principles. The Candidate does not yet specify which clauses move, which remain normative elsewhere and which are informative summaries.
2. **Problem-Space Sufficiency sharpens an existing invariant.** Prior requirements and the archived Formation method already protected upstream prerequisites and the correct scope. The current baseline retains that intent, but the preventive relation is less explicit. A source clarification is supported; a diagnosed causal explanation for recent behavior is not.
3. **DL coverage is insufficient as the entire non-regression test.** Conditional risk/recovery, consequential state divergence, Human capability effects, interaction calibration and portability are only partly represented. These can be repaired within existing families and owners.
4. **The carrier map is a proposed allocation, not a bound realization.** It conflates semantic owner, carrier and executor in several rows. The Candidate itself wisely denies runtime proof, but its future use requires a clearer distinction.

### Problem-Space Sufficiency: exact diagnosis

The independent lineage is:

- Requirements CR-01 distinguishes proposed means, need, outcome and constraints.
- CR-02 protects claim-relative scope, parent contribution and “current focus” boundaries.
- CR-10 prohibits omission of material upstream prerequisites.
- CCR-01 preserves open framing; CCR-04 addresses higher purpose and competing resources.
- Target Architecture §5.3 protects upstream readiness before downstream work.
- Archived Formation requires a missing basis that could materially change route, use, quality, method, maturity or authority.
- Native Work Transition already defines qualified handoff sufficiency and explicitly allows unresolved questions that Work can handle within the contract. [RQ], [TA], [WF], [NWT]

Therefore the primary classification is **UNDER-SPECIFIED EXISTING INVARIANT**, with **activation/application failure as a plausible, unproven realization hypothesis**. It is not genuinely absent canonical knowledge. Nor can this review conclude it was already behaviorally adequate.

The Candidate's causal phrasing in J §9 and F/FCR-03 goes beyond available evidence when it treats the preventive activation gap as the established explanation of recent failures. The request supplies a credible observed failure pattern, but neither it nor the Candidate binds the underlying episodes to exact effective instructions, available evidence and first divergence. Candidate narrative repetition does not fill that gap. Static clarification can proceed without falsely settling the RCA.

**Recommended replacement semantic, not a CI payload:**

> Before substantial optimization or commitment, establish enough connection between the local work, its relevant Parent Outcome and material causal dependencies to justify the next investment. If a specific unresolved premise could credibly change the problem boundary, causal relevance, decision level, route or solution space, resolve the decision-relevant part or contain the uncertainty through bounded probing, staging or an explicitly weaker claim. Preserve qualified state; reopen only affected dependencies. Do not require exhaustive upstream understanding, pre-solve questions delegated to Work or broaden ordinary bounded work without a material reason.

This allows problem–solution co-evolution. A cheap mockup may be the best way to discover the problem; it is not prohibited “solution work.” An authorized local contribution may also remain useful despite uncertainty in the wider project. The standard is enough justification for the next investment, not certainty about ultimate success.

## E. Work Functions: recommended set

The Candidate's eleven functions are largely complete as a semantic inventory. Their overlap is not inherently wrong: a prototype can create evidence, and evaluation can expose a new frame. The danger is turning the list into routing logic or treating every name as a separate owner.

Recommend nine descriptive entries. Preserve refinement as an explicit sub-transformation of product development; move closure to a cross-cutting outcome of work rather than a final stage.

| Recommended function | Transformation / original coverage | Execution ownership and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Frame and explore** | Form purpose, problem, constraints and viable directions; includes sensemaking, hypothesis formation and contrastive calibration | Native Chat or Work, or narrower domain method. May remain the requested product itself. |
| **2. Acquire and qualify evidence** | Recover, research, measure, experiment, reconcile and synthesize evidence | Native research/tools/provider; sustained work in Work. A frame may change as evidence changes. |
| **3. Analyze a decision** | Develop a recommendation for a bound choice | decision-analysis or narrower method; Human/domain decision rights remain separate. |
| **4. Design, create and refine** | Strategy, mechanism, artifact creation and transformation to next-use maturity | Native Chat for bounded work, Work and professional production methods for sustained work. Refinement changes the product; assurance evaluates it. |
| **5. Form executable work** | Turn selected direction into activities, dependencies, method/craft, context, authority and return boundaries | NWT owns material pre-handoff sufficiency; native Work owns the actual operational plan, sequencing and adaptation. |
| **6. Execute and integrate** | Perform authorized actions and integrate contributions into parent work | Native Work/tools/apps/providers; bounded direct action may remain in Chat. Child completion does not close parent work automatically. |
| **7. Assure a claim** | Check exact property/readiness/use claim with failure-capable basis | Narrower evaluator preferred; EWP where differentiated. No automatic completion gate. |
| **8. Transition into use** | Prepare receiving context, access, adoption/action, dependencies and recovery | Domain process, Human owner and authorized Work; delivery alone does not establish use. |
| **9. Observe, evaluate and adapt** | Obtain performance/outcome evidence, update scoped beliefs/work and propose change | Domain measurement/evaluation; system-specific change to System Learning/system-development. Outcome attribution needs its own basis. |

**Close, wait, stop, hand off, repair or reopen can result from any function.** They are not a mandatory final “Learning/Closure” phase.

No missing transformation justifies an additional universal function. Strategy/portfolio management is conditionally covered by framing, decision and design with strategic authority retained externally. Persistent operating capability management belongs to the system/domain owner, not every Work episode. Teaching belongs to conditional Human capability outcomes, not another default function. Coordination and state integrity constrain all functions.

Operational planning, decomposition, sequencing, tool/provider selection, integration and ordinary local repair remain native Work responsibilities for substantial agentic work. The local contract constrains outcome and validity-critical method, not every operational move. Existing NWT's allowance for Work to resolve research/design questions prevents a pre-handoff formation bottleneck. [NWT]

### Minimal realization discipline

For a material carrier change, the existing compilation method already provides the needed procedure:

1. Bind the exact accepted source and target surface/version.
2. For each affected obligation identify embedded wording, an actually bound external owner, a verified native mechanism, an explicit unknown or non-applicability.
3. Preserve trigger → contribution → integration/claim boundary, not just the semantic noun.
4. Check only relevant activation and behavior through genuine work before making those claims. [SD-COMP]

An example: “NWT owns the bridge” is a source statement. A context-complete handoff actually included in the receiving Work request is a concrete carrier. Work using the method/evidence/return constraints is execution evidence. This review's explicit handoff and selected system-development method establish those events only for this review, not general spontaneous Chat→Work behavior.

**Redundancy rule:** a compact runtime cue and a richer method can coexist because they serve different activation/detail roles. Two independently authored general policies do not gain value merely by repeating one another. A source pointer alone must never be labeled DELEGATED + BOUND without evidence of access at the point of need.

### What belongs where

**Global CI:** compact, stable cooperation preferences with plausible cross-context value—direct answers and useful joint thinking; quality before simplification; qualified reuse; relevant recovery and continuity; Human agency and material-change legibility; evidence/fallback integrity; proportional completion. Problem-Space Sufficiency is eligible only as a conditional cue, not a global discovery script. These are eligible contents, not an instruction to insert every item or reopen PR #60.

**Local prompts / Work Objects:** actual Parent Outcome, deliverable, recipient, sources, accepted state, hard constraints, uncertainty that changes the route, authority envelope and success criteria. Include a specific method, tool or process when validity or the user's explicit requirement depends on it.

**Projects:** continuing relevant context and genuinely scoped behavioral constraints. Do not create a full duplicated cooperation kernel merely because Project instructions have precedence. Inspect the needed effective behavior and preserve only material local rules/overlaps; explicit source identity can prevent drift during later edits.

**Native Work:** operational plan, decomposition, sequencing, provider/tool selection, iterative production, integration and local repair within the authorized contract. Validity-critical methods and protected commitments constrain its freedom; they do not require a universal function router.

**Skills / methods:** repeatable differentiated transformations and professional techniques. Decision analysis and EWP can activate within larger work; they do not own the surrounding episode. Prefer a narrower qualified provider when it fully owns the task. [DA], [EWP], [REG]

**Native behavior without custom carrier:** ordinary answering, explaining, reasoning, routine sequencing, ordinary tool integration, routine context selection, basic formatting and direct bounded work. Maintain canonical expectations as review criteria without automatically prompting them all.

### Specific prompt risks

- “Resolve uncertainties before optimizing” can force research when staging or a reversible probe is sufficient.
- “Before execution, develop the problem with the Human” can pull AI-resolvable formation out of Work and create unnecessary questions.
- “Human judgment is required” can become universal permission-seeking unless tied to non-substitutable or protected decisions.
- Repeated “do not mutate / ask first” language can interfere with already authorized ordinary work.
- Globally enumerated function names invite classification and process narration instead of useful output.
- “Use a qualified method” remains weak if the method cannot be discovered or the selected source is only mentioned.
- Global brevity can suppress needed evidence or recipient-ready completeness.
- A full canonical document loaded into every task can obscure the actual task and introduce competing imperatives.

These are design failure hypotheses; they were not measured against an installed Candidate during this review. The remedies are conditional wording, local specificity and evidence-based reduction.

### External architecture references: bounded transfer

NASA's requirements guidance supports validating lower-level requirements against stakeholder expectations, assumptions, objectives and solution form, and checking redundancy. It supports the purpose–problem traceability concern; it does not justify importing NASA's full process into everyday joint work. [NASA]

Microsoft's Human–AI interaction research treats behavior during use, errors and adaptation over time as design concerns. This independently supports treating coordination as more than output formatting; it does not prove the Candidate's exact CJS-09 wording or require an interface layer. [HAI]

The broader CWA, SSM, value-focused thinking and complementarity references listed in J are not needed to decide this bounded source/ownership repair. Their stronger theoretical or empirical claims are not adopted here. This report is not a claim of formal CWA, ISO, safety or systems-engineering conformance.

### Portability

The cooperation core and function definitions are mostly portable. The current map is intentionally OpenAI-specific. Separate those two claims: another vendor can realize the same obligations differently, without reproducing Chat/Work/Projects/Skills one-for-one.

Preserve a conditional requirement that important decisions, methods and state remain exportable or reconstructable when switching/recovery risk matters. That is a design requirement; this review has not tested export completeness or another vendor's behavior.

# Rebaseline Assessment — 2026-09-02

**Status:** CANDIDATE EVIDENCE — not architecture, not runtime authority, not promoted state

## Controlling question

What demonstrably contributes to good professional Human–AI work, and what is merely a custom implementation hypothesis that may be better handled by current ChatGPT native capabilities, installed providers, professional methods, or ordinary tool use?

## Why this exists

Real-use failures repeatedly showed a gap between coherent system design and actual work-product quality: hallucination/plausibility substitution, premature convergence, professional incompleteness, generic output, intent/constraint loss, regression, context/state loss, authority confusion, method/provider/surface failures, craft defects, assurance failure, false completion, Human-as-QA, process substitution, overengineering, maintenance drift, and meta-work capture.

This assessment does not design a replacement architecture. It requalifies existing customization against current native capability and genuine-use evidence.

## Durable principles retained as candidate learnings

- Outcome before means: a request or proposed solution is evidence of intent, not automatically a complete specification.
- Reality and epistemic integrity: distinguish fact/observation, inference, assumption, judgment, forecast/scenario, conflict and unknown when material.
- Coherence is not completeness: professional work may be coherent yet omit material dimensions, methods, failure modes, recipient/use context, craft or evidence.
- Professional sufficiency: general model fluency does not substitute for an adequate professional method, evidence basis, standard, reference or specialist capability where material.
- Human agency: preserve Human values, judgment, authorship, authority, responsibility, acceptance and commitment where material.
- State/authority integrity: conversation, memory, working state, accepted knowledge and authoritative state are not interchangeable.
- Claim-relative assurance: produced != good; reviewed != verified; generated != fit for intended use.
- Minimum sufficient work: perform the smallest work that reaches the relevant sufficiency floor without omitting material prerequisites.
